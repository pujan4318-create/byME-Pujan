Nepali Script, Roman Nepali, or English: Which Keywords Should You Target?

By Pujan, November 6, 2026

There is no single right answer for every topic. English, Roman Nepali, and Devanagari searches can each bring different customers, so the safe method is to test all three with your own data before choosing.

The three forms

FormExampleWatch out for
Englishwebsite design in NepalCompetition from big and international sites
Roman Nepaliwebsite banaune tarikaMany spellings for the same word
Devanagariवेबसाइट बनाउने तरिकाTools may show thinner data

These are example phrases to show the difference, not phrases I am claiming have a certain volume.

How to decide, step by step

1. Type each version into Google

Note the autocomplete suggestions and look at the results. Are the same pages ranking for all three, or does each version show different sites and answer types? Different results mean different competition and different chances.

2. Compare in Google Trends

Compare the versions with the region set to Nepal. Devanagari terms may show little data. That does not mean nobody searches them, only that the tool has less to show.

3. Check your own Search Console queries

If your site is already live, the Performance report shows which form real visitors used to find you. This is the most reliable evidence you will get.

4. Decide by audience and by what you can write well

Write in the language your readers would expect from your business. A tourism company, a school, and a software service may have very different audiences.

5. Test one page, then review

Publish one page in your chosen form and review Search Console after a few weeks. Keep the test small and change one thing at a time.

How to write pages for each form

  • Use one language per page. Do not mix scripts in the same sentence just to catch keywords.
  • Set the page language correctly. Nepali pages use lang="ne".
  • Use hreflang only when you have genuine equivalent pages in different languages.
  • Put the phrase naturally in the title, the first paragraph, and a heading.

What I do not know

I do not have verified data on which form wins for a given topic. It depends on the audience and the subject. Anyone who gives one answer for every business is guessing. Test, measure, and keep what the data supports.
